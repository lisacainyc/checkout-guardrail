🛡 OptOut is installed

Shop like normal. Skip the bill.

With OptOut on, you can browse, fill your cart, and click "Place order" like you always do. Instead of buying, you get an order confirmation and see how much money you kept. When you really want to buy something, turn OptOut off.

1. Pin OptOut to your toolbar

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Find OptOut and click the pin next to it.

The OptOut icon now stays in your toolbar, so you can always see if it's on.

2. Use the switch

Click the OptOut icon to open the switch.

  • ON Orders on supported stores are stopped. Green "Protected" labels show on the buy buttons.
  • OFF Everything works normally and orders are real. Use this when you mean to buy.

OptOut starts out on. After a real purchase, remember to switch it back on. The icon always shows which state it's in: a green shield when on, a red shield when off.

3. Know where it works

OptOut can stop orders on these stores:

    On any other store's checkout, a red banner warns you: "OptOut can't protect this checkout. Orders here are real." If a buy button doesn't have a green "Protected" label, assume the order is real.

    Your privacy

    • OptOut never reads payment info. It never reads what you type into a page: no card numbers, addresses, or anything else you enter in a form.
    • Nothing leaves your browser. Your switch setting and your list of stopped orders are saved only on this computer. OptOut has no server and sends no data anywhere.

    Why OptOut asks for these permissions

    Read and change your data on all websites
    This is the warning Chrome shows at install. OptOut needs it for two jobs: stopping the buy buttons on supported stores, and spotting checkout pages on other sites so it can warn you. On a page, it only looks at the page address, buy buttons, item names, prices, and the order total.
    Storage
    Saves your switch setting, your stopped orders, and the total you've kept, on this computer only.
    Active tab
    When you click the OptOut icon, it checks the address of the page you're on, to tell you whether that store is protected.
